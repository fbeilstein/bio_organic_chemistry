**Who can photosynthesize?**

</div>

<div style="position: absolute; left: 16.07%; top: 3.81%; width: 80.35%; height: 93.44%;">

**Oxygenic Photosynthesizers**
Use H₂O as an electron **donor **→ release O₂
**Pigment**: chlorophyll a
**Product**: glucose (C₆H₁₂O₆)
**Reaction**: 6CO2 + 6H2O −[light]-> C6H12O6 + 6O2

**Organisms**:
Plants — chloroplasts derived from cyanobacteria
Green algae (Chlorophyta)
Red algae (Rhodophyta)
Cyanobacteria — only prokaryotes that perform oxygenic photosynthesis
*Chloroplasts are descendants of an ancestral cyanobacterium engulfed ~1.5 billion years ago.*

**Anoxygenic Photosynthesizers**
Use other **donors **(e.g. H2S, Fe2+, organic acids) → no O₂ produced
**Pigments**: bacteriochlorophylls (absorb infrared light)

**Organisms**:
Purple sulfur bacteria (Chromatium) — donor: H2S
Purple non-sulfur bacteria (Rhodospirillum) — donor: organic compounds
Green sulfur bacteria (Chlorobium) — donor: H2S
Green non-sulfur bacteria (Chloroflexus) — donor: organic acids
Heliobacteria — Gram-positive phototrophs, rare in soils
*Anoxygenic photosynthesis predates oxygenic forms — it evolved first, before Earth’s atmosphere contained O₂.*
