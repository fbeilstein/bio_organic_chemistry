**Problem 2. **Promoter binding assay.

<div style="position: absolute; left: 16.07%; top: 20.32%; width: 80.35%; height: 71.57%;">

You have a protein X that you suspect should sequence-specifically bind to a promoter DNA. You radiolabel the promoter DNA from the gene you suspect to be regulated by X. Than you perform Electrophoretic Mobility Shift Assay (<u>Wikipedia</u>).

You run 5 lanes:
No X protein
1 mg/ml X protein (fair enough to bind some DNA)
5 mg/ml X protein (more than enough to bind all almost DNA)
5 mg/ml X protein + a lot of primer for the same promoter (competitive)
5 mg/ml X protein + some primer-sized random DNA

Draw what you expect to see after developing the radiograph in case your assumption is true.
