<div style="position: absolute; left: 15.80%; top: 20.96%; width: 80.35%; height: 66.05%;">

Most information about the environment we get from visual stimulæ
More than 50% of the cerebral cortex is involved in visual processing
Vision is highly contextual, brain integrates previous experience to build a model of the world
We rather see with brain than eyes
Saccadic masking: the brain selectively blocks visual processing during eye movements in such a way that neither the motion of the eye (and subsequent motion blur of the image) nor the gap in visual perception is noticeable to the viewer.
