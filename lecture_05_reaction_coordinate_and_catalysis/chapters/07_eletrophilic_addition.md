A schematic free-energy versus reaction coordinate diagram for the 1,2- and 1,4-addition of HBr to 1,3-butadiene. An allylic carbocation is common to both
pathways. The energy barrier for attack of bromide ion on the allylic cation to form the 1,2-addition product is less than that to form the 1,4-addition product. The 1,2-addition product is **kinetically **favored. The 1,4-addition product is more stable, and so it is the **thermodynamically **favored product.
