**The pH optima of some enzymes.**
 
Pepsin is a digestive enzyme secreted into gastric juice, which has a pH of ∼1.5, allowing pepsin to act optimally.
Trypsin, a digestive enzyme that acts in the small intestine, has a pH optimum that matches the neutral pH in the lumen of the small intestine (6.1 to 7.0 in the duodenum, 7.5 distal ileum).
Alkaline phosphatase of bone tissue is a hydrolytic enzyme thought to aid in bone mineralization
