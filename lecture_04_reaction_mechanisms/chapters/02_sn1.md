**Hyperconjugation** involves electron delocalization (via partial orbital overlap) from a filled bonding orbital to an adjacent unfilled orbital. In the case of a carbocation, the unfilled orbital is the vacant p orbital of the carbocation, and the filled orbitals are C−H or C−C sigma bonds at the carbons adjacent to the
p orbital of the carbocation.
