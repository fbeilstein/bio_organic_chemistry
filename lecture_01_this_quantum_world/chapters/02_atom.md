T**he orbital angular momentum quantum number**, l, determines the angular
momentum of the electron as it moves in its orbital. This quantum number tells us the shape of the orbital, spherical or whatever. The values that l can take depend on the value of n: l can have any value from 0 up to n – 1. The different possible values of l are given
letters rather than numbers and they are called s, p, d, and f.

</div>

![](./lecture_01_this_quantum_world/images/slide_29_img_28.png) {left=16.07 top=24.65 width=61.46 height=21.67}

The **magnetic quantum number**, ml, determines the spatial orientation of the angular momentum. Its value depends on the value of l, varying from –l to +l. The different possible values of ml are given suffixes on the letters.

</div>

**Aufbau principle / ****Madelung rule**
The lowest-energy orbitals fill up first, according to the order 1s -> 2s -> 2p ->
3s -> 3p -> 4s -> 3d. Note that the 4s orbital lies between the 3p and 3d orbitals in energy. (Note: energy grows with n+l and with n if n+l are equal, e.g. 3s = 3+0=3 and 2p=2+1=3, but 2p is lower because 2<3)

**Pauli exclusion principle**
Only two electrons can occupy an orbital, and they must be of opposite spin.

**Hund’s rule**
If two or more empty orbitals of equal energy are available, one electron occu-
pies each with spins parallel until all orbitals are half-full
